Local guide

Rimini to discover

Our tips for making the most of Rimini, a short walk from the apartment.

🏖️

Sea & beach

  • Rimini seafront promenade — newly renovated, perfect for a stroll
  • The beach, 50 metres from the apartment
  • Parasol and sunbed hire right on the beach
  • Sunset over the sea from the balcony — not to be missed
🏛️

Art & culture

  • Arch of Augustus — a Roman monument from the 1st century BC
  • Tiberius Bridge — 2,000 years old and still in use
  • Malatesta Temple — a Renaissance jewel
  • City Museum — the story of Rimini in one route
🍽️

Where to eat

  • Lo Zodiaco — fresh fish since 1991, a few steps from the apartment 🌟
  • Dalla Lella — Romagna piadina made the traditional way (winner of Bastianich's Foodish programme)
  • Da Guido 1946 — ⭐ one Michelin star, inventive cooking rooted in tradition
  • Rimini covered market — street food and local produce
🚴

Sport & activities

  • Seafront cycle path — 15 km of route
  • Running along the seafront at sunrise or sunset
  • Beach volleyball and beach tennis on the sand
  • Bike hire — a few steps from the apartment
🎡

Things to do

  • Fiabilandia — amusement park, 5 minutes away
  • Italia in Miniatura — a one-of-a-kind attraction
  • Acquafan — one of the largest water parks in Europe
  • Oltremare — nature and dolphin park
  • Nightlife — Rimini is famous across Europe
🚗

Getting here

  • Rimini airport (RMI) — 5 minutes by taxi
  • Miramare station — 7 minutes on foot
  • Rimini main station — 10 minutes by bus (line 11), Metromare or taxi
  • A14 motorway — Riccione exit

Before you book

Frequently asked questions

The things guests ask us most often. If you can't find what you need, just write to us.

How far is the beach?

Fifty metres, a one-minute walk. The apartment looks onto Rimini's new seafront promenade in the Miramare area, and you can see the sea from the balcony and from the double bedroom.

How many people can stay?

Two to four. There is a double bedroom and a small room with bunk beds. Guests not listed in the booking cannot stay overnight.

Is parking included?

It depends on the season. From May to September a reserved parking space is included at Via Mantova 32, two minutes away by car, in an area with CCTV and on-site staff, accessed with a key card handed over with the keys. From October to April the reserved space is not active: you park freely on the street or, free of charge, in the marked area on the opposite side of the building.

Is there a lift?

No. The apartment is on the second floor of a quiet building and is reached on foot. Worth knowing in advance if you travel with heavy luggage or have difficulty with stairs.

Is it suitable for families with children?

Yes. There are beach toys, board games, colouring sets and children's books, and a cot and high chair are available on request. The beach fifty metres away and the pedestrian promenade make the area easy with small children.

Is there a washing machine?

Not in the apartment. There is a self-service laundry, Clean Island, a few minutes away in Via Costantinopoli.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 3:00 pm to 7:00 pm, check-out by 11:00 am. For later arrivals, self check-in with a key box is available on request. Final cleaning is included.

How do I get to the apartment?

Miramare station is a seven-minute walk; Rimini's main station is fifteen minutes by car or ten by bus on line 11. Rimini airport is five minutes away by taxi. The number 30 bus stop is fifty metres away, in front of the building.

How do I book?

By filling in the form above. Lisa replies within a few hours confirming availability and details, and payment is arranged directly with her. An ID document is required at check-in.
