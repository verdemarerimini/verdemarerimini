Privacy notice

This page explains how personal data is handled when you visit the Verde Mare website or send an availability enquiry. It is provided under Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679. The individual cookies are listed in the cookie policy.

Who handles the data

The data controller is Lisa Berlini, acting as a private individual, at Viale Pomezia 2, 47924 Rimini (RN), Italia. For any question or request concerning personal data, write to info@hostrimini.it.

What data is collected

Through the availability form: first name, surname, email address, phone number, arrival and departure dates, number of guests and any notes you choose to write.

If the enquiry becomes a stay, identity document details are also collected on arrival, as required by Italian law.

Browsing the site involves no profiling and no marketing or advertising cookies. The typefaces are hosted on our own domain and the map in the contact section is loaded only if you ask for it with the button provided: opening the site therefore downloads nothing from external servers.

If you consent to statistical cookies, Google Analytics collects browsing data: pages viewed, length of visit, device and browser type, language, approximate location and referral source. This data is used in aggregate form and does not serve to identify you.

Cookies and usage statistics

On your first visit a banner asks whether you accept statistical cookies. Until you choose, no statistics tool is activated; if you decline, none is activated at all.

The tool in use is Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-X28S3D0D0P), provided by Google Ireland Limited. It serves only to understand how many people visit the site and which pages they read, so that it can be improved. It is not linked to any advertising product and is not used to build individual profiles.

Google uses the IP address to derive an approximate location and does not retain it in the Analytics data. Retention of user-level and event-level data is set to two months, the shortest period the tool allows.

You can change your mind at any time: the Cookie preferences link at the bottom of every page reopens the choice. Withdrawing consent stops the collection and deletes the Analytics cookies present on your device.

Why it is processed, and on what basis

To answer your enquiry and arrange a possible stay. The legal basis is the performance of pre-contractual and contractual measures, Article 6(1)(b). Without this data we cannot reply.

To comply with legal obligations. In the case of a stay, the reporting of guest details to the public security authorities and tax and administrative requirements. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(c).

For site usage statistics. The legal basis is your consent, Article 6(1)(a) of the Regulation, together with Article 122 of the Italian Personal Data Protection Code for the storage of cookies. Consent is freely given and can be withdrawn: the site works in exactly the same way if you decline.

Who else sees the data

Data is never sold or passed to third parties for commercial purposes. It is processed, for their respective technical functions, by the following providers:

  • Formspree (Formspree Inc., United States) — collects and forwards messages sent through the form.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., United States) — hosts the site's pages and records technical access data.
  • Google (Google Ireland Ltd. and Google LLC, United States) — provides Google Analytics, active only with your consent, and the map in the contact section, loaded only if you press the button. In both cases Google receives the device's IP address. Without consent and without a click on the map, the site does not contact Google's servers.
  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd.) — only if you choose to contact us through that channel.

Some of these providers are based in the United States: transfers take place under the standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission or under an adequacy decision.

How long it is kept

Enquiries that do not lead to a booking are kept for twelve months and then deleted. Data relating to an actual stay is kept for the periods required by tax and public security rules.

Statistical data collected by Google Analytics, if you have consented, is kept for two months at user and event level. The cookies Analytics leaves on the device have their own lifetime, shown in the cookie policy.

Your rights

You may at any time ask to access your data, to correct it, to erase it, to restrict its processing, to receive it in a readable format or to object to the processing. Simply write to info@hostrimini.it: you will receive a reply within thirty days.

If you have consented to statistical cookies you may withdraw that consent at any time through the Cookie preferences link, without having to write to anyone. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing already carried out.

If you believe the processing does not comply with the law, you may lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority.

Updates

Any changes are published on this page, with the date updated at the bottom. Please check it from time to time.
