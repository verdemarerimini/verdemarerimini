Cookie policy

This page lists the cookies and similar technologies used by the Verde Mare website, what they are for and how long they last. The wider picture on data handling is in the privacy notice.

In short

The site uses no advertising or profiling cookies. Without your consent no statistics tool is activated and no content is loaded from external servers: the typefaces are hosted on our own domain and the map in the contact section appears only if you ask for it with the button provided.

You can change your choice at any time through the Cookie preferences link at the bottom of every page.

Necessary cookies and storage

These are needed for the site to work and to remember your choice. They require no consent and cannot be switched off from the banner, because without them the choice would have to be made again on every visit.

NameTypePurposeLifetime
vm_cookie_consent_v1 Browser local storage (localStorage), not a cookie: it is never sent to any server. Remembers whether you accepted or declined statistical cookies, so the banner does not reappear. Stays until you clear the site's data in your browser.

The site itself sets no other technical cookies. GitHub Pages, which hosts the pages, records technical access data in its own logs but sets no cookies.

Statistical cookies (only with your consent)

These are set by Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-X28S3D0D0P) only after you have pressed “Accept” or enabled the corresponding option under “Customise”. They serve to count visits and to see which pages are read.

NameSourcePurposeLifetime
_ga Google Analytics, set on our own domain Distinguishes the devices visiting the site, so the same person is not counted twice. 2 years from being set or last updated
_ga_X28S3D0D0P Google Analytics, set on our own domain Keeps the session state for this particular Analytics property. 2 years from being set or last updated

Some Google configurations may also set short-lived cookies named _gid or _gat, lasting less than a day. If present on the device, they are deleted along with the others when you withdraw consent.

On withdrawal the site stops the collection and deletes the Analytics cookies readable from our domain. Any cookies Google may set on its own domains are beyond our reach and must be removed, if you wish, from your browser settings.

The data collected is kept by Google for two months at user and event level. Google's information on how the data is used: policies.google.com.

Managing cookies from your browser

Whatever choice you made on this site, every browser lets you view, block or delete the cookies already stored: the setting is under privacy or website data. Clearing the site's data also removes the record of your choice, and the banner will reappear on your next visit.
